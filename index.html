Constitution & By-Laws

of the IT-GIRLS Sorority

ISÓTITA TELOS — Equality is the Goal
ENTER THE CHARTER ↓
00 · PREAMBLE

United by purpose.

We, the members of the IT-GIRLS Sorority, united by the values of friendship, academic excellence, leadership, service, integrity, and personal growth, hereby establish this Constitution and By-Laws to guide our organization.

We aim to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment where every member can develop their abilities, contribute to the community, and build meaningful friendships.

THE CONSTITUTION

Articles of Governance

⌕

Section 1. The name of this organization shall be IT - GIRLS, hereafter referred to as the Sorority.

Section 2. The Sorority shall have an official emblem, colors, and motto approved by its members.

Section 3. Isótita Telos (Ισότητα Τέλος) meaning "Equality is the Goal" - IT girls also means a popular, attractive, and fashionable young woman who captures public attention and sets cultural trends. She has an unexplainable charm—the "It" factor—that makes everyone notice her.

IOTA Even the smallest things matter.
TAU Balance and stability.
  1. To promote academic excellence among its members.
  2. To develop leadership, communication, and organizational skills.
  3. To encourage friendship, cooperation, and mutual respect.
  4. To participate in community service and school activities.
  5. To promote responsible citizenship and positive values.
  6. To provide members with opportunities for personal and professional development.
  7. To maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all members.
  8. To empower and inspire among the members.

Section 1. Eligibility. Membership shall be open to qualified students who meet the requirements established by the Sorority and the school.

Section 2. Membership shall be divided into the following categories:

01 · ACTIVE

Members who regularly participate in meetings and activities.

02 · INACTIVE

Members who temporarily cannot participate because of valid academic, personal, or other reasons.

03 · HONORARY

Individuals recognized for significant contributions to the Sorority or community.

Section 3. No member shall be discriminated against based on personal background, beliefs, abilities, or circumstances.

Section 4. Membership shall be voluntary, and no student shall be pressured into joining or remaining in the organization.

The officers of the Sorority shall consist of:

01 President
02 Vice President
03 Secretary
04 Treasurer
05 Auditor
06 Public Relations Officer
07 Sergeant-at-Arms

President

  1. Serve as the chief student leader of the Sorority.
  2. Preside over meetings.
  3. Coordinate organizational activities.
  4. Represent the Sorority when necessary.
  5. Ensure that this Constitution and By-Laws are followed.

Vice President

  1. Assist the President.
  2. Assume the President's responsibilities when the President is unavailable.
  3. Coordinate committees and projects.
  4. Monitor the progress of organizational activities.

Secretary

  1. Record minutes of meetings.
  2. Maintain organizational documents.
  3. Prepare meeting notices.
  4. Keep an updated membership record.
  5. Handle official correspondence.

Treasurer

  1. Maintain accurate financial records.
  2. Collect authorized contributions or membership fees.
  3. Prepare financial reports.
  4. Safeguard organizational funds.
  5. Ensure that expenditures are properly approved.

Auditor

  1. Review the financial records of the Sorority.
  2. Verify financial reports.
  3. Ensure that funds are properly accounted for.
  4. Report financial concerns to the organization and faculty adviser.

Public Relations Officer

  1. Promote approved Sorority activities.
  2. Manage official announcements.
  3. Prepare publicity materials.
  4. Maintain a positive public image of the organization.

Sergeant-at-Arms

  1. Help maintain order during meetings.
  2. Assist with attendance.
  3. Help organize meetings and events.
  4. Promote a respectful environment.
  1. Elections shall be conducted once every school year.
  2. Any active member who meets the organization's requirements may run for office.
  3. Elections shall be conducted fairly and confidentially.
  4. The candidate receiving the highest number of votes shall be declared elected.
  5. In case of a tie, a second round of voting shall be conducted between the tied candidates.
  6. Officers shall serve for one academic year unless otherwise provided by the school.
VISION

To shape a generation of confident women who recognize their worth, challenge inequality, and use their influence to create opportunities that extend beyond themselves.

To make equality more than an ideal, but a culture that lives through every member, every generation, and every opportunity created for others.

MISSION

To cultivate an inclusive sorority environment where every member is valued, empowering individuals through academic collaboration, alumni mentorship, and career growth, while driving unity and shared resources to create a lasting community impact.

  1. All organizational funds shall be used only for legitimate Sorority activities and projects.
  2. All expenses shall be documented and approved according to the organization's procedures.
  3. Financial records shall be available for review by authorized members and the faculty adviser.
  4. No member shall personally benefit from Sorority funds.

Members who violate the Constitution, By-Laws, school policies, or the rights of other members may be subject to disciplinary action.

Possible disciplinary measures include:

  1. Verbal or written warning
  2. Temporary suspension from activities
  3. Removal from an organizational position
  4. Termination of membership
ZERO TOLERANCE

Hazing, bullying, harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or any activity that places members at risk shall be strictly prohibited.

Any disciplinary action shall follow fair procedures and applicable school policies.

  1. The Sorority may be dissolved if membership becomes insufficient or if the organization can no longer fulfill its purpose.
  2. Dissolution shall require approval by at least two-thirds of active members and the appropriate school authority.
  3. Remaining organizational funds and property shall be handled according to school regulations and shall not be distributed among members.
THE BY-LAWS

The working rules.

Practical procedures that keep the Sorority organized, fair, safe, and accountable.

BY-LAW I

Membership Procedures

  1. Applicants shall complete the required membership process.
  2. Applicants shall agree to follow the Constitution and By-Laws.
  3. Membership shall be confirmed after meeting the organization's requirements.
  4. Members are expected to maintain respectful behavior and satisfactory participation.
BY-LAW II

Attendance

  1. Members should attend regular meetings whenever possible.
  2. Members who cannot attend should provide a valid reason.
  3. Repeated unexcused absences may result in a warning or loss of active-member privileges.
  4. Academic responsibilities shall take priority over Sorority activities.
BY-LAW III

Conduct

  1. Treat everyone with respect.
  2. Avoid bullying, harassment, and discrimination.
  3. Follow school rules.
  4. Protect the reputation of the organization.
  5. Resolve disagreements through respectful communication.
  6. Never use intimidation or coercion against another member.
  7. The Sorority shall not conduct hazing or dangerous initiation activities.
BY-LAW IV

Election Procedure

  1. The election date shall be announced in advance.
  2. Candidates may present their qualifications and plans to members.
  3. Voting shall be conducted confidentially.
  4. Votes shall be counted by designated election officers.
  5. Results shall be announced after verification.
  6. Newly elected officers shall undergo a proper turnover of responsibilities.
BY-LAW V

Financial Procedure

  1. All collections shall be recorded.
  2. Receipts or other appropriate documentation shall be maintained.
  3. Expenses must be approved before funds are released.
  4. The Treasurer shall provide regular financial reports.
  5. The Auditor shall review financial records.
  6. Organizational funds shall never be used for personal expenses.
BY-LAW VI

Events & Activities

  1. Have a clear educational, social, leadership, or service purpose.
  2. Receive the necessary school approval.
  3. Follow school safety regulations.
  4. Respect the rights and well-being of participants.
  5. Have designated officers or members responsible for organization and supervision.
BY-LAW VII

Faculty Adviser

  1. Guide the organization in following school policies.
  2. Provide advice to officers and members.
  3. Help ensure that activities are safe and appropriate.
  4. Review major projects when required.
  5. Assist in resolving serious organizational concerns.
BY-LAW VIII

Order of Business

01 Call to Order
02 Attendance
03 Reading and Approval of Previous Minutes
04 Officer Reports
05 Committee Reports
06 Old Business
07 New Business
08 Announcements
09 Open Forum
10 Adjournment
FINAL PROVISION

Adoption

This Constitution and By-Laws shall become effective upon approval by the members of the IT-GIRLS Sorority and the appropriate school authority.
